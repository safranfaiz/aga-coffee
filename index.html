A quiet stop on the 27A road.

Aga Coffee sits just off the highway in Galle, with a roastery next door and a terrace in the shade. People come for the cappuccino, iced mocha and matcha, and stay for the pastries and gelato.

Breakfast, a mid-day break or a slow afternoon with friends. It is calm, clean and easy to park.
